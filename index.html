      About ATSInsight

      What it is

      ATSInsight is a free, frontend-only resume builder and ATS analyzer built with HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript.

      Why ATS compatibility matters

      Many employers use applicant tracking systems to parse and filter resumes. Clear headings, standard structure and relevant keywords help your resume be read correctly.

      How scoring works

      Out of 100: contact 10, sections 15, keyword match 25, formatting 15, content quality 20, readability 15. Every check is a transparent rule in script.js.

      Privacy

      Your resume stays in your browser. ATSInsight does not send your resume data to a server. Data is stored only in localStorage on your device.

      Limitations

      Rules cannot judge meaning or quality of experience. PDF and DOCX text extraction is not supported offline; paste your text instead.

      Disclaimer: ATSInsight provides an estimated ATS compatibility score based on rule-based checks. Actual ATS systems vary by employer and software.
